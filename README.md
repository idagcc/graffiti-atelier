# Graffiti Atelier

Graffiti Atelier is a fullscreen TouchDesigner painting app. Visitors choose from six colors and paint directly on the 3D car or a wall. Press **Tab** for operator settings, including the surface, paint finishes, drips, and six-point LiDAR calibration.

Download the [TouchDesigner project](Graffiti%20Atelier/Graffiti%20Atelier.toe) and open it in TouchDesigner. See the [setup guide](Graffiti%20Atelier/START%20HERE.txt) for SLAMTEC RPLIDAR S3 and FrameGrabber.NET setup.
